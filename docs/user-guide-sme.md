# HaliCred SME User Guide

## Welcome to HaliCred

HaliCred is an AI-powered green credit scoring platform that helps Small and Medium Enterprises (SMEs), farmers, and informal workers access affordable loans by proving their climate-friendly practices. This guide will walk you through using the platform to upload evidence, earn green scores, and apply for loans.

## Getting Started

### Account Registration

1. **Visit the HaliCred Platform**
   - Web App: https://app.halicred.com
   - Mobile App: Download from app store

2. **Create Your Account**
   - Enter your phone number (e.g., +254123456789)
   - Select "SME" as your user type
   - You'll receive an OTP via SMS

3. **Verify Your Phone Number**
   - Enter the 6-digit OTP code
   - Your account is now active

### Business Profile Setup

After verification, complete your business profile:

1. **Business Information**
   - Business name
   - Business type (agriculture, manufacturing, transport, services)
   - Location (city/region)
   - Business size (number of employees)
   - Annual revenue range

2. **Contact Details**
   - Primary contact person
   - Email address (optional)
   - Physical address

3. **Consent Preferences**
   - Data sharing permissions
   - AI processing consent
   - Carbon credit participation
   - Marketing communications

## Understanding Green Scores

### What is a Green Score?

Your Green Score is a number from 0-100 that reflects your business's environmental sustainability. Higher scores lead to:
- Better loan terms and lower interest rates
- Access to green financing options
- Carbon credit earning opportunities
- ESG compliance recognition

### Green Score Components

Your score is calculated based on four pillars:

1. **Energy Efficiency (25%)**
   - Renewable energy usage (solar, wind, biogas)
   - Energy-efficient equipment (LED lights, efficient appliances)
   - Energy conservation practices

2. **Waste Management (20%)**
   - Waste reduction initiatives
   - Recycling programs
   - Composting systems
   - Circular economy practices

3. **Sustainable Practices (25%)**
   - Sustainable sourcing
   - Environmental certifications
   - Green business operations
   - Supply chain sustainability

4. **Carbon Footprint (30%)**
   - Emission reductions
   - Carbon-neutral initiatives
   - Climate impact mitigation
   - Transportation efficiency

### Typical Score Ranges by Sector

- **Agriculture**: 45-85 points
- **Manufacturing**: 40-80 points
- **Transport**: 35-75 points
- **Services**: 50-90 points

### How to Upload Evidence

1. **Navigate to Evidence Section**
   - Click "Upload Evidence" from the dashboard
   - Or go to "My Evidence" section

2. **Select Evidence Type**
   - Choose the appropriate category
   - Select specific evidence type
   - Add description (optional but recommended)

3. **Upload Your File**
   - Take a clear photo or upload document
   - Supported formats: JPG, PNG, PDF
   - Maximum file size: 20MB
   - Ensure text is readable

4. **Submit for Processing**
   - Review your submission
   - Click "Process Evidence"
   - AI analysis begins automatically

### Photo Guidelines for Best Results

#### Receipt/Invoice Photos
- **Good lighting**: Use natural light or bright indoor lighting
- **Flat surface**: Place receipt on flat, contrasting background
- **Full document**: Include all edges and corners
- **Clear text**: Ensure all text is readable
- **No blur**: Hold camera steady to avoid motion blur

#### Equipment Photos
- **Multiple angles**: Take photos from different perspectives
- **Labels visible**: Ensure model numbers and specifications are clear
- **Installation context**: Show equipment in its installed location
- **Before/after**: Include photos of old equipment being replaced

#### Common Photo Issues to Avoid
- ❌ Blurry or out-of-focus images
- ❌ Poor lighting (too dark or bright)
- ❌ Partial documents cut off
- ❌ Reflective surfaces creating glare
- ❌ Very small or distant objects

### AI Processing

After uploading, our AI system:

1. **Analyzes your evidence** using computer vision and OCR
2. **Extracts key information** like equipment type, capacity, cost
3. **Calculates environmental impact** using emission factors
4. **Verifies authenticity** against known patterns
5. **Updates your Green Score** based on sustainability impact

Processing typically takes 30-60 seconds. You'll see:
- Processing status updates
- Confidence score (how sure the AI is)
- Impact on your Green Score
- Carbon credits earned

## Applying for Loans

### Loan Eligibility

Your loan eligibility depends on:
- **Green Score**: Higher scores unlock better terms
- **Business profile**: Sector, size, and revenue
- **Evidence quality**: Well-documented sustainability practices
- **Financial history**: Previous loan performance (if any)

### Getting a Loan Quote

1. **Navigate to Loan Section**
   - Click "Apply for Loan" from dashboard
   - Or go to "Loan Opportunities"

2. **Enter Loan Details**
   - Desired loan amount
   - Purpose (equipment, working capital, expansion)
   - Preferred term (12, 24, 36 months)

3. **Review Your Quote**
   - Interest rate (influenced by Green Score)
   - Monthly payment amount
   - Green discount applied
   - Total cost of loan

### Loan Application Process

1. **Pre-Application Check**
   - Verify your Green Score is current
   - Ensure business profile is complete
   - Upload any additional required evidence

2. **Submit Application**
   - Accept loan quote terms
   - Provide business plan/purpose statement
   - Upload financial documents (if requested)
   - Agree to terms and conditions

3. **Bank Review Process**
   - Banks review your AI-verified profile
   - Green Score provides credibility
   - Typical review time: 3-5 business days

4. **Loan Decision**
   - Approval/rejection notification
   - Final terms confirmation
   - Disbursement timeline

## Carbon Credits

### Understanding Carbon Credits

Carbon credits represent verified reductions in greenhouse gas emissions. Each credit equals one tonne of CO2 equivalent reduced or avoided.

### How You Earn Credits

1. **Evidence Processing**: AI calculates emission reductions from your evidence
2. **Verification**: System validates reduction calculations
3. **Credit Allocation**: Credits assigned to your account
4. **Aggregation**: Credits pooled with other SMEs for trading

### Types of Activities That Earn Credits

#### High-Impact Activities (0.5-2.0 credits/year)
- Solar panel installations
- Electric vehicle adoption
- Biogas digester systems
- Energy-efficient manufacturing equipment

#### Medium-Impact Activities (0.2-0.5 credits/year)
- LED lighting upgrades
- Water conservation systems
- Waste reduction programs
- Sustainable transport initiatives

#### Low-Impact Activities (0.1-0.2 credits/year)
- Recycling programs
- Energy conservation practices
- Sustainable sourcing
- Green office initiatives

### Credit Value and Trading

- **Current market value**: $5-15 per credit
- **Minimum trading amount**: 10 credits
- **Trading frequency**: Quarterly
- **Payment timeline**: 30 days after trade

## Common Issues and Solutions

### Evidence Upload Issues

#### Problem: Photo Not Clear Enough
**Solution**:
- Retake photo with better lighting
- Use flat surface with contrasting background
- Ensure all text is readable
- Try different angles if needed

#### Problem: Receipt/Document Rejected
**Solution**:
- Check if document shows required information
- Ensure date, amount, and supplier are visible
- Add description explaining the sustainability aspect
- Contact support if issue persists

#### Problem: Low Confidence Score
**Solution**:
- Upload additional supporting evidence
- Provide more detailed description
- Include before/after photos
- Submit related documentation

### Green Score Issues

#### Problem: Score Not Updating
**Solution**:
- Wait 24 hours for processing to complete
- Check if evidence was successfully processed
- Verify evidence meets scoring criteria
- Contact support if delayed beyond 48 hours

#### Problem: Lower Score Than Expected
**Solution**:
- Review score breakdown by pillar
- Upload additional evidence for weak areas
- Ensure evidence quality meets standards
- Focus on high-impact activities

### Loan Application Issues

#### Problem: Loan Application Rejected
**Solution**:
- Improve Green Score with more evidence
- Complete business profile fully
- Provide additional financial documentation
- Consider smaller loan amount

#### Problem: Higher Interest Rate Than Expected
**Solution**:
- Upload more evidence to increase Green Score
- Ensure all sustainability initiatives are documented
- Consider longer-term relationship building
- Explore green loan programs

### Technical Issues

#### Problem: Cannot Log In
**Solution**:
- Check phone number format (+254XXXXXXXXX)
- Verify SMS reception for OTP
- Clear browser cache/cookies
- Try different browser or device

#### Problem: App Running Slowly
**Solution**:
- Check internet connection
- Close other apps/browser tabs
- Clear app cache (mobile)
- Try during off-peak hours

#### Problem: File Upload Failing
**Solution**:
- Check file size (max 20MB)
- Verify file format (JPG, PNG, PDF)
- Try smaller/compressed version
- Use different internet connection

### Glossary

**Carbon Credit**: A tradeable certificate representing one tonne of CO2 equivalent reduced or avoided

**Green Score**: A 0-100 rating of your business's environmental sustainability

**Emission Factor**: A coefficient that quantifies emissions per unit of activity

**OCR**: Optical Character Recognition - technology to extract text from images

**AI Processing**: Automated analysis of evidence using artificial intelligence

**Sustainability Pillar**: One of four categories used to calculate Green Score

**ESG**: Environmental, Social, and Governance criteria for business sustainability

**SME**: Small and Medium Enterprise
